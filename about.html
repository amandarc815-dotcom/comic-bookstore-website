About Sir Sammy's Comic Kingdom

About Us

From the very beginning, our comic store has always prioritized the needs and desires of our customers. We' re thrilled to say that Sir Sammy's Comic Kingdom Store has expanded with branches in several states, bringing joy to even more communities. Our main store is in Charlottesville, Virginia, and we're excited to have our first branch in Houston, Texas, along with two more locations in Florida. While each location may have its own unique charm, our commitment to creating a welcoming and fun atmosphere remains the same. We're eager to introduce board game nights, cosplay, and other engaging activities in all our branches, ensuring everyone has a chance to connect and enjoy these experiences together. Furthermore, we're proud to offer a wide range of comic books, costumes, and the best board games through our online store, making it easy for you to find something that brings you joy, no matter where you are.
