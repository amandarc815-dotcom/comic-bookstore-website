Our History

Sir Sammy's Comic Kingdom Comic Book Store began its journey in 2002, a time filled with both dreams and challenges. Like many, we faced our fair share of setbacks, but instead of giving up, we chose to adapt and evolve, striving to create a more vibrant and welcoming comic world for our community. Through persistent efforts and all the work we put into it, we’ve been humbled to witness our store grow into a beloved haven for comic enthusiasts. In 2008, we received the honor of being named the best comic store, a recognition that opened doors to a wealth of beloved resources and connections. And in 2015, we proudly earned a certificate of honor, a testament to the hard work and dedication of our team.
Over the years, we have cherished the relationships we've built with our loyal customers and the travelers who visit from near and far. Our journey has been about more than just selling comics; it has been about creating a community where everyone feels included and appreciated.
