Board game night

Every week, the bookstore hosts a lively board game night, welcoming both novice and experienced players. Attendees can enjoy a variety of genres and classic games, and everybody is encouraged to bring your games to share with others. This event is more than just a gaming session; it’s an opportunity for everyone to come together, play games, and enjoy some pizza in a fun and friendly atmosphere.

Time and place

Sunday 6:00PM-10:00PM, 432 Comic Street, Charlottesville, Virginia 87653
