Cosplay superhero party



On the 20th of every month, we will host a superhero cosplay party. If you or your friends love superheroes, come join us and wear your favorite superhero costumes as we celebrate comics and superhero culture together. The event will feature cosplaying competitions and opportunities to interact with other superhero enthusiasts. Winners in the competitions will receive a free comic book as a reward.

Time and place

The 20th of every month, 5:30PM-8:30PM, 213 Comic Island, Houston, Texas 11111
