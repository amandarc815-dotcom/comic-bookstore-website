Biography of Lucy

As the founder and manager of the bookstore, Lucy oversees all aspects of its operations. Her passion for comic books drives her commitment to providing customers with an exceptional reading experience. In her daily tasks at the comic bookstore, Lucy manages inventory and supervises staff, while also organizing various events to attract more comic fans. She believes in the comic bookstore not just as a retail space, but as a vibrant social venue that offers people a fun place for leisure and entertainment.

Personal Info
