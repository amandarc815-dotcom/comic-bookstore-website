Biography of Maria

Maria loves dealing with planning and organizing various activities at the bookstore. She has extensive experience in event planning and is passionate about creating engaging events. Maria excels at designing activities that appeal to comic lovers of all ages, intending to attract more customers to the comic bookstore. She believes in her mission to enhance the bookstore's profitability while providing entertaining experiences that bring happiness to readers, achieving a double-win outcome. Additionally, she places a strong emphasis on interactivity and fun when planning activities.
