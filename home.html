We provide entertaining activities and variety of unique comic books for readers.

Our comic bookstore has created an inviting space filled with a comic atmosphere for all types of comic lovers. In addition to showcasing our extensive comic collection, we regularly host a variety of exciting entertainment activities. Every Sunday night, also, we also offer board game nights and superhero cosplay parties. Additionally, we provide backroom rentals for live entertainment events, such as photography exhibitions and short video shoots, which highlight cosplay styles.
Our goal is to create a warm, comfortable, and enjoyable environment where every visitor can find their moments of happiness.

If you find yourself passionate about comics and feel that life is lacking excitement, or if you're looking for a fun place to unwind with friends and enjoy some cosplay and game adventures, this is just the spot for you. We understand how important it is to have a space where you can connect with your interests and share joyful moments with others. If you are interested in this, please follow us to discover fun activities and become a part of our community!
